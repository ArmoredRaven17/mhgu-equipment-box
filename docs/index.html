About / Attributions

An unofficial, non-commercial fan tool for planning your MHGU equipment box. The layout mirrors the game: 2000 hunter slots and 1000 Palico slots, in pages of 100.

Equipment data: by way of the mhgu-editor project's data files, which derive from Kiranico.

English item, skill and equipment names: MHGUDB-iOS (MIT) & gatheringhallstudios community database. The game files store names in Japanese only, so the English naming throughout this tool comes from their work.

Equipment icons: monsterhunterwiki.org — licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0; adaptations shared under the same licence.

Menu textures and font: carried over from the MHGU Collection Tracker.

Monster Hunter content is owned by Capcom. This tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by Capcom.
